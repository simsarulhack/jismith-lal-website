About Me

A Different Angle
to Life & Success

I'm Jismith Lal — a Trainer, Mentor, and Motivational Coach with a deep passion for helping people discover their true potential. With advanced degrees in Mathematics and a heart for teaching, I bring a unique analytical and human-centered approach to coaching.

After five years of research and mentoring countless students — especially NEET aspirants — I've developed a proven system that helps individuals turn aspirations into reality. My journey spans from the classroom to the boardroom, empowering both students and entrepreneurs alike.
